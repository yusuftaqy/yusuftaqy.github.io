Commissioned by the **Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM)** in partnership with **INDEF**, this study mapped Indonesia's bauxite-to-aluminium value chain to understand where domestic value is created — and where it leaks out of the country.

### What the study did

Using multi-source trade and production data, the project traced each stage of the chain — from bauxite mining, to alumina refining, to aluminium smelting — and estimated the domestic value added at each step. The goal was to give policymakers an evidence base for strengthening downstream processing (*hilirisasi*) rather than exporting raw ore.

### My role

- Mapped the bauxite-to-aluminium **value chain** across production and trade stages.
- Analysed multi-source data to locate where domestic value is **gained or lost**.
- Reviewed the **final report** and the **ministerial presentation** delivered to ESDM.

### Key outputs

- A value-chain map of Indonesia's bauxite–aluminium sector.
- Estimates of domestic value added at each processing stage.
- Policy recommendations on downstream processing and industrial strategy.
